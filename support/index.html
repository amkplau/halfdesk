Common questions

Where are my records stored?

Only on your iPhone (and in your iPhone’s backups). Halfdesk has no account and no cloud copy.

How do I keep a backup?

In the app, go to Settings → Back up now, and save the file somewhere safe such as iCloud Drive in the Files app. Halfdesk reminds you once a month.

I’m not getting reminders.

Check iPhone Settings → Notifications → Halfdesk and make sure notifications are allowed. Open Halfdesk at least once every two weeks so reminders stay scheduled.

How do I delete my data?

Settings → Delete all data in the app, or delete the app. See the privacy policy.

Is the deduction figure tax advice?

No. It’s an estimate from your records. See the terms.
