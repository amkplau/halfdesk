Privacy policy

Last updated 1 Nov 2026

In short: your work-from-home records stay on your iPhone. Halfdesk has no accounts and no servers that hold your data. We never see your hours, dates or times.

Halfdesk is provided by AT MOBILE KITCHEN PTY LTD (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information.

What Halfdesk stores on your iPhone

  • Your schedule (which days you usually work from home, your usual hours and lunch break).
  • Your daily records: the date, start and end times, and whether time was at home or at the office.
  • Your settings, such as reminders and app language.

This information is stored only on your device, shared with the Halfdesk widget on the same device, and included in your iPhone’s own backups if you have backups turned on. Halfdesk does not upload it anywhere.

Exports

When you export a CSV or PDF report, or save a backup, the file is created on your device and saved or shared only where you choose (for example, the Files app). We don’t receive a copy.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which features are used, Halfdesk sends anonymous usage events through TelemetryDeck, for example “a day was confirmed” or “a report was exported”, with simple details such as how many days were added. These events never include your hours, dates, times, notes or any personal details. TelemetryDeck uses an anonymised identifier that cannot be used to identify you, and the data is not used for advertising or to track you across other apps and websites. See TelemetryDeck’s privacy policy.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification data is sent to us.

No tracking, no selling

Halfdesk has no ads, does not track you, and does not sell or share personal information.

Deleting your data

Go to Settings → Delete all data in the app, or delete the app. Because your records never leave your device, there is nothing for us to delete on a server. Anonymous usage statistics can’t be linked back to you.

Children

Halfdesk is made for working adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
